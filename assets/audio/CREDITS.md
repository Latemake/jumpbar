# Jumpbar sound credits

All source recordings are CC0 1.0: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
Clips are trimmed, converted to 24 kHz mono PCM WAV and peak-normalized. Runtime layering, pitch variation, spring and whistle synthesis are Jumpbar sound design.
